Tales of Destiny

Tales of Destiny is a 2D top-down roguelite survival game developed in GDevelop, inspired by Vampire Survivors. Players battle endless waves of enemies, gain experience to level up, and choose from a variety of skills and upgrades to create unique builds each run. With progressively increasing difficulty and randomized upgrade choices, the game encourages strategic decision-making and high replayability as players strive to survive for as long as possible.

Main Menu

The central hub featuring Play, Settings, and Exit controls for a seamless gameplay experience.

Game Lobby

A 2-player online lobby system developed in GDevelop, allowing players to host or join rooms and synchronize before gameplay begins

Level-up System & Abilities

A level-up system that lets players choose from random abilities and upgrades, allowing unique builds every run.

Boss Battle System

A timed boss system where a powerful boss appears after two minutes. Defeating the boss unlocks a portal to the second stage.

Leaderboards

A leaderboard that records players who successfully complete the game, ranking them based on their performance.
